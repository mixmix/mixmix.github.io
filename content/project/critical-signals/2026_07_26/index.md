date: 2026-02-01

2025 was made possible through a lot of input from a heap of talented people,
but the main weaver was Sophie Jerram. She did an amazing job, and the project
would not exist without her.

Coming into 2026 the crew met to discuss the long-term vision of the project, and decided
we wanted to restructure the project. We invested a lot in building governance and processes
which would allow us to distribute decisions making + authoriy more effectively.

It was pretty challenging, and if I'm honest a bit painful, but I'm excited to see
how it changes the course and longevity of this work.
