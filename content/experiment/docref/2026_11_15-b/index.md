The overview of changes was interesting, but words changed isn't a reliable signal
of impact of those changes. Thinking about ways to measure or reveal that in the dataset
I got to wondering whether we could detect contention around an act - perhaps the signal
would be changes and reversions by successive governments, or intense editing of the
same stretches of legislation.
