About the data

  • Versions and change counts come from legislation.docref.nz, which publishes 70 New Zealand Acts and regulations (a selection, not the whole statute book) with every consolidated version. Each version's changelog.json lists the provisions (“blocks”) added, deleted, amended in place and renumbered compared with the previous version. Data fetched on 15 November 2026.
  • A dot is placed on the version's date, which is usually when amendments came into force. The amending law may have been passed months or years earlier, sometimes by a previous government. Treat the government colouring as “in force under”, not “passed by”.
  • The source does not record which bill made each change, or who sponsored it, so this page can't show which party proposed an edit. Most amendments are government bills, but members' bills and cross-party changes also happen.
  • Hollow rings mark the earliest version DocRef holds for that document. For older Acts this is usually the first online consolidation (around September 2007), not the date the Act was passed. “From enactment” draws a dashed line back to the year in the Act's title.
  • “Substantive edits” counts blocks added, deleted or amended. “+ renumbering” also counts renamed blocks. Renumbering can be large but mechanical.
  • Governments are shown by the leading party, with coalition and support partners in the thin strip below it. The current National-led government is shown from 27 November 2023; this page does not reflect the result of the 2026 general election.
