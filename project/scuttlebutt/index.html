Project

Scuttlebutt

A p2p protocol + community

Oct 01, 2015

⏳ 2015 – ongoing

This is the project I have contributed the most to in my life. It’s a p2p replicating database, and a solarpunk community that grew up around and through it. I’ve made life-long friends and built bridges spanning almost every continent and honestly it’s a challenge hard to summarize.

🚧 Please feel free to direct queries to me about what you’d like to know more about
